Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous cybersecurity experience?
Answer:

No. We welcome both experienced professionals and motivated beginners with strong technical foundations and a passion for cybersecurity.

Is remote work available?
Answer:

Yes. Depending on the position, we offer remote, hybrid, and on-site opportunities to fit different work preferences.

What qualifications are required?
Answer:

A background in Computer Science, Information Technology, Cybersecurity, or a related field is preferred, but equivalent skills and experience are also valued.

Which technical skills are most important?
Answer:

Knowledge of networking, Linux, scripting, ethical hacking, operating systems, and problem-solving are highly desirable for most roles.

How long does the hiring process take?
Answer:

The recruitment process typically takes between one and two weeks, including application review, technical assessment, and interview.

Do you provide training for new employees?
Answer:

Yes. Every new team member receives onboarding, mentorship, and access to continuous learning resources and professional development.
